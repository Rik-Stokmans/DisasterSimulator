# NL Resilience Lab

An interactive first-72-hours disaster simulator for the Netherlands. V3 combines official municipality, buurt and safety-region geography, real Dutch road and public-transport geometry, and a deterministic multi-hazard dependency model whose assumptions and causal traces remain visible and editable.

Hosted preview (Cloudflare Access): [rikstokmans.com/warroom/projects/disasterSimulator](https://rikstokmans.com/warroom/projects/disasterSimulator)

## What is in this prototype

- 342 Dutch municipalities on an interactive MapLibre map with zoom, pan and selection, plus switchable PDOK cartography, annual 25 cm aerial orthophotos, a sun-synchronised Realistic view and official nationwide 3D terrain/buildings loaded only at city scale.
- 11 Windy-inspired map modes: overview, hazard, electricity, drinking water, drainage, gas, telecom, mobility, critical facilities, population needs, and emergency supply.
- An independent, timeline-aware affected-region overlay that remains nationwide when the user zooms back out and stays visible while the user changes map question, basemap or 2D/3D perspective. It distinguishes affected, critical and unsupported/Unknown municipalities instead of treating missing spatial evidence as unaffected.
- A 72-hour timeline with 289 quarter-hour states, scrub/wheel controls, events, stepping, playback, and 1×/4×/12× speeds.
- Ten scenario presets: severe storm, pluvial flood, river flood, coastal flood, heatwave/drought, winter storm, cyberattack, Groningen earthquake, wildfire/smoke, and a compound cascade.
- A municipality-by-municipality evidence audit. Every completed `correction-required` or blocked audit is compiled into a deterministic fail-closed guard before a production build: its unsupported local consequence, access, outage and recovery precision is withheld across all hazard presets, while source geometry and diagnostic values can remain visibly non-operational. A stale or missing generated guard blocks the build. Hydraulic/coastal eligibility, source containment and other physical corrections still require explicit human review; the generator never turns audit prose into a causal model.
- Season, KNMI’23 climate path/horizon, intensity, event duration, preparedness, response delay, an optional validity-aware reference date, and winter/summer A/B comparison controls.
- Automatic scale transition: zoom into a municipality to lazy-load its real CBS buurten, BAG woonplaatsen, and TOP10NL town/village/hamlet footprints; zoom out to return to the national model. The 342 municipality and 342 settlement bundles are deterministically gzip-compressed for runtime delivery, while their uncompressed checked-in forms remain available to validation and audit tooling.
- A road topology graph built from real NWB junction IDs. It is restricted to Mobility and explicitly assumed road logistics/Supply; it never substitutes for power, water, drainage, gas, telecom or facility topology. The map preserves multiple A/N-road alternatives between municipalities and draws exact RWS geometry instead of centroid-to-centroid lines. Municipality-border continuations are partitioned by NWB's primary physical road number, and generation fails if one source section would create distinct parallel corridors for the same border; legitimate consecutive series reuse remains explicitly non-additive. Contributing section IDs, signed and administrative road identities, and road-part classes remain provenance: Eindhoven's audited A2/RW2 OPR sections join the existing A2 crossing rather than creating a false independent route named `002`; Goeree-Overflakkee's eastern crossings display their retained signed A29 identity while keeping A4/RW4 as provenance; and N307/Markermeerdijk/Markerwaarddijk fragments across the Houtribdijk share one audited physical corridor and one capacity ceiling.
- Real ProRail traces/stations and current OVapi/NDOV-derived bus, tram, metro, rail, and ferry shapes.
- Audited lifelines add Holwerd–Ameland, TESO Den Helder–Texel, Kessel–Reuver, Bergen–Vierlingsbeek and the three Horst aan de Maas–Venlo vehicle ferries (Broekhuizen–Arcen, Lottum–Lomm and Velden–Grubbenvorst), plus Capelle–Krimpen’s N210/Algerabrug and the exact NWB N630/Turnhoutsebaan crossing between Goirle and Tilburg, to Mobility and Supply only. The three new Maas crossings use exact BRT TOP10NL mixed-traffic ferry features; the nearby bicycle-only feature is deliberately not promoted to a vehicle lifeline. Operator load and maximum-vehicle weights, recurring hours and the status observed on 3 September 2026 are retained with provenance. Because the human-readable outage notices publish no issue time, expiry, stable API or update cadence, they remain non-causal context and cannot silently close an unbound T+0–T+72 route. Physical tonnes never become model throughput; all causal route throughput remains generalized scenario units. The Goirle route keeps Belgian N12/Poppel/Turnhout continuation, A58 access and event-time passability as Unknown until compatible valid-time evidence is bound. Dordrecht’s N3 Papendrechtsebrug carries a sourced 17 July 2026–21 April 2027 full closure that reaches zero road/logistics scenario capacity only for an overlapping reference date. Gorinchem’s A27 Merwedebrug record instead preserves its published vehicle classes: trucks/touring coaches are restricted while cars, scheduled buses and emergency services remain allowed; without a public vehicle mix it stays visible, validity-bounded context and never deletes the entire edge. Krimpen’s Algerabrug record keeps the 10 August–7 September 2026 ordinary-motor closure, controlled emergency lane, temporary active-mode bridge and Waterbus mitigation distinct; the date-only exercise field cannot resolve the 05:00 endpoint and no unsupported numeric capacity is invented. Leiden–Leiderdorp’s Lage Rijndijk/Spanjaardsbrug is represented by its exact NWB centreline chain and official 17 August–11 September 2026 car-and-bus closure, with emergency/cycle/moped passage separate; because no defensible route capacity or modal mix is published, the gray crossing remains non-causal context. Leudal’s N280 Kelperbrug restriction is attached only to the exact Weert–Leudal corridor, separates heavy/agricultural/non-scheduled buses from scheduled public transport and emergency services, and expires at retrieval because the official notice gives no end date; empty NDW never means open. Lingewaard’s 24 August–2 October Rijndijk closure is attached to exact internal NWB sections and shown as gray Mobility/Supply context with the N838 detour; missing modal, emergency, queue and throughput evidence keeps it non-causal. Lisse’s A44 main carriageway, Lisserweg underpass and local connection remain separate lifecycle states: the date-only 6 September reopening is planned, never silently confirmed. The N216 Schoonhoven–Gelkenes crossing is now correctly typed from its NWB VDA sections as a vehicle ferry rather than a 150-unit road. Its published timetable and vehicle capability remain visible, but capacity and scenario-time operation are Unknown, so the context-only connection is excluded from causal transfers.
- A server-refreshed NDW DATEX II current-road feed with a separately toggleable **Traffic + accidents** overlay. It distinguishes `Accident`, congestion (`AbnormalTraffic`) and other incident/management records, exposes the feed publication time, refreshes through a same-origin Gatekeeper adapter every 60 seconds while in use, and still appears automatically on Mobility and Supply. A checked-in deployment snapshot is an explicitly labelled fallback. Both live and fallback records are observed context—not a 72-hour traffic forecast—and do not yet alter scenario routes.
- A client-safe future traffic-camera adapter: camera inventory, online/offline state and media availability are separate fields; any eventual imagery must use an allow-listed same-origin proxy with observation/expiry time and an approved privacy/licence review. No authorized public feed is currently configured, so the empty overlay is explicitly Unknown rather than “zero cameras”.
- Rijkswaterstaat FIS-VNDS fairway lines plus scale-aware bridge and lock register points on Mobility and Supply, requested from the live CC0 ArcGIS MapServer. These establish static inland-waterway and crossing context only: they are not navigability, restriction, operating state, vessel throughput or a T+72 forecast. The separate `vaarwegstatus` codes describe route roles—not open/closed state—so live Notices to Skippers remain a distinct future adapter.
- A deployment-time KNMI 5-minute precipitation-radar rendering on severe-storm, pluvial-flood and compound Hazard maps. One national WMS image is cached server-side, visibly timestamped, and remains current observational context rather than a scenario-aligned 72-hour forecast.
- A deployment-cached KNMI Seamless ensemble-probability overlay for the same precipitation Hazard maps. The timeline shows a real 1 km probability of exceeding 3 mm/h at the nearest T+1, T+3 or T+6 checkpoint, preserving reference/valid time, threshold, licence, image hash and NoData semantics. At T+0 the map shows radar; after T+6 it shows no forecast raster and explicitly labels the missing horizon Unknown rather than extrapolating it to T+72. Both products use explicit EPSG:3857 requests because the current WMS advertises transposed CRS:84 bounds; this prevents valid-looking empty PNGs.
- KNMI's near-real-time 10-minute in-situ observations as a source-timestamped Hazard overlay: 55 European-Netherlands/offshore stations, with 42 inside a CBS municipality polygon. Storm/coastal/winter views show gust, pluvial/river views show preceding-hour rain, and heat/fire views show temperature. Every station retains its WSI, interval-end time and missing variables; fixed points are never interpolated into municipal weather, projected through T+72 or applied to scenario forcing automatically.
- KNMI's continually issued 48-hour weather-warning product on official CBS 2025 province polygons. Hazard view outlines provinces by their highest published code yellow/orange/red and retains phenomenon, validity period and source timestamp. The connector prefers the structured Open Data API; if its shared anonymous quota is unavailable, it may establish only an explicit timestamped national “no warnings” result from KNMI's own page. Any other failure remains Unknown. Warnings never alter an unrelated simulation.
- A deployment-time KNMI FDSN seismic-event catalogue on earthquake Hazard maps. The current 365-day fixture contains 63 earthquake or induced-event origins with magnitude, depth, review state and event time; 11 blasts/sonic booms are excluded. These points are observed source parameters, not shaking, damage, probability, forecast or causal scenario input, and absence in a municipality is never treated as zero hazard.
- A deployment-time RIVM Luchtmeetnet snapshot on wildfire/smoke Hazard maps: the latest indicative PM2.5 reading at 72 reporting stations in the six-hour retrieval window, with available PM10, NO2, ozone and SO2 retained. These are visibly timestamped, revisable point observations—not a continuous air-quality surface, population exposure estimate, plume, source attribution, forecast or scenario input. Municipalities without a mapped station remain no-data, never zero.
- IPO/Risicokaart Public Domain forest, heath and dune polygons on wildfire Hazard maps. Their catalogue metadata was updated 19 February 2026, but they remain combustible-area eligibility context—not an ignition, fuel/moisture state, incident perimeter, spread forecast, evacuation zone or response state; missing geometry is Unknown rather than safe.
- RVO/PDOK 2025 definitive crop-declaration parcels at city scale on drought/fire Hazard and Supply maps. The annually declared 15 May snapshot provides crop category/name/code and parcel geometry, not current crop condition, fuel, yield, food stock, damage or T+72 supply. The UI preserves the service's differing permissive reuse statements (dataset metadata Public Domain Mark 1.0; WMS capabilities CC0) instead of silently choosing one.
- A 63,567-record public HWH/PDOK IMWA and DAMO water-asset snapshot—surface-water pumps, weirs, sluices, sewer pumps and wastewater-treatment plants—plus city-scale DAMO/GWSW wastewater-pipe WMS layers, lazy-loaded on Water and Drainage as official context. Each point is assigned, where unambiguous, by containment in the full-resolution CBS 2025 municipality boundary; outside/shared-boundary points stay explicitly unassigned and never mean absence. The 22 records explicitly marked `niet meer aanwezig` remain in the provenance snapshot but are excluded from the default map; every other register status still is not proof of live operation.
- A deployment-time Rijkswaterstaat Waterinfo snapshot with 310 latest water-level and 65 latest discharge observations. Water and relevant flood Hazard maps retain each station's observation time, official class, vertical datum, freshness and possible-fault flag; stale records remain warnings rather than disappearing. The data is CC0 unless individually stated otherwise and remains non-causal point context, not a hydraulic surface, breach, flood-depth map or scenario-aligned forecast.
- A separate deployment-time Rijkswaterstaat Waterinfo forecast snapshot with 673 issued point values across requested +1, +6, +12, +24, +48 and +72-hour windows. The Water map follows the exercise timeline to the nearest cached checkpoint and keeps water level and discharge visually distinct from observations. The current source returns 145/144/144/144/52/0 water-level points and 11/11/11/11/0/0 discharge points across those windows; empty +48/+72 quantities remain explicit NoData, never zero or safe. Each point retains requested window, valid time, unit, parameter identities, class, possible-fault flag, municipality containment, response time/hash and CC0 terms. Because the rolling endpoint publishes no single model-cycle issue time, this is official forecast context but is not assumed to align with exercise T+0 and does not drive the simulation.
- A live Rijkswaterstaat/Climate Impact Atlas WMS raster on pluvial Hazard maps showing modeled inundation-depth classes for a national 70 mm-in-two-hours cloudburst. The CC0 layer is official scenario context—not current weather, a selected incident, a municipal stress test or a T+72 forecast—and remains non-causal; transparent/NoData coverage is Unknown rather than dry.
- Rijkswaterstaat/PDOK AHN4 ground elevation at city scale on flood Hazard maps. The 0.5 m CC0 DTM was acquired in 2020–2022 and filters non-ground objects; it is terrain context only, never current water, flood depth, flow direction, passability or scenario forcing.
- HWH/PDOK IMWA drainage/supply and level areas on Water/Drainage, plus current water-defence geometry and Rijkswaterstaat risk areas based on the 2018 report / ROR second cycle 2016–2021 on river/coastal Hazard maps. The risk-area dataset has no planned refresh; these are context layers, not a selected current hydraulic scenario or proof of defence performance.
- WMCN/LIWO 2025.1.6 candidate primary, regional, regional-water-system and outside-dike breach locations on river/coastal Hazard maps from city zoom. They are entry points for choosing a compatible named scenario—not active failures, probabilities, flood depths or T+72 forecasts—and never affect the simulation without explicit scenario selection. The public service's unstated reuse-licence version remains visible.
- 2,885 KVK/PDOK public-order, safety and public-administration service locations, lazy-loaded on the Facilities map as coarse official context.
- All 79 RIVM 2026 emergency-department questionnaire locations on Facilities, BAG-geocoded from the published addresses: 76 report 24/7 opening and 71 report acute obstetrics. Published room/2025-visit responses remain dated context and never become live incident capacity.
- 12,883 active DUO RIO education-location uses in the European Netherlands map scope, refreshed from four daily CSVs and filtered across use, relation and recognition validity dates. The Facilities map distinguishes statutory education frameworks while explicitly making no shelter, attendance, staffing, supply or capacity claim.
- Current Liander/Alliander CC BY 4.0 electricity and gas geometry: all 7,201 published high-voltage cable/station records load on demand, while dense medium/low-voltage and gas records are queried only for the visible extent at publisher-appropriate zoom levels. The same bounded query path now adds Stedin's July 2026 electricity lines and gas-replacement programme. Stedin states free reuse and annual publication but does not name a standard licence; its gas layer is not a complete gas network. Both operators remain partial, non-causal context.
- Latest numeric hourly GTS physical-flow directions from the ENTSOG Transparency Platform on Gas: the deployment snapshot retains observation period, point identity, direction, kWh/h unit, provisional status, true zero, missing/N/A count, extraction date and source hash. Virtual, aggregated, storage, LNG, production, distribution and border records are deliberately not summed or geocoded; ENTSOG terms govern reuse and no standard open-data licence is claimed. The feed is national transmission context, not municipal pressure, customer supply, outage telemetry or a T+72 forecast, and never alters the simulation.
- Netbeheer Nederland electricity-capacity planning context sampled at one public DUO postcode in every municipality: 339 of 342 lookups currently resolve to 250 published service areas. Power-map marker colour represents the returned TenneT withdrawal constraint class and halo size the sampled large-user withdrawal queue. The snapshot is non-causal and deliberately does not claim complete municipal coverage, outage state, physical topology, current load, switching, emergency reserve, restoration or a T+72 forecast; the publisher states the data product is open data but no exact dataset licence identifier was found.
- Public-domain Stichting Mijn Aansluiting provider territories on the Power, Gas, Water, Drainage and Telecom maps, plus a live point lookup that identifies the responsible provider(s) at the selected municipality. These areas are responsibility context, not physical topology or live service coverage.
- 32,270 deduplicated public antenna-installation sites from the statutory RDI Antenneregister, representing 67,508 registered antennas across mobile, fixed-link, broadcast, other-mobile and radio-amateur uses. The Telecom map loads this deployment snapshot on demand; it is infrastructure context, not radio coverage, operator ownership, traffic, backhaul, power/backup or live availability. The public WFS states no fees/access constraints but does not state a reuse-licence version, which remains explicit in the UI.
- Live public Register Externe Veiligheidsrisico's WMS context: regulated activities plus fire, explosion and toxic-cloud attention areas on Hazard, hazardous-substance pipeline references on Gas, and Basisnet hazardous-goods locations on Mobility/Supply. The register layers never imply a current incident, plume, shipment, outage or modeled effect; the WMS's unstated fixed cadence, feature time and reuse-licence version remain explicit.
- A source → real corridor → municipal gateway → CBS buurt allocation graph. Transfers conserve delivered units, respect effective route capacity, and expose direction, utilization, failures, and bottlenecks.
- Cascading dependencies: power constrains water pumping, drainage, telecom, gas controls, and critical facilities; mobility constrains emergency supply.
- Three response policies for comparison: baseline, protected critical generators, and early municipal mutual aid.
- Structured “why” traces with signed contributions, dependencies, backup windows, model drivers, exact what-if checks and explicit explanation methods.
- Advanced sensitivity controls for clock/day type, demand, backup endurance, logical-network capacity, repair capacity, household preparedness, climate overrides, event focus and compound components.
- A background-worker analytics workspace with all 60 model outputs over 72 hours and distribution graphs for all 36 official CBS buurt fields.
- Layer-specific hazard and service-flow animation with a fully static reduced-motion mode.

Geography and transport geometry are official/open-data context. Utility networks, failures, repair curves, resource quantities, infrastructure capacity, and response outcomes are scenario assumptions. They must not be interpreted as forecasts or operational advice.

## Run locally

Requirements: Node.js 22 and pnpm 11.

```bash
pnpm install
pnpm data:refresh
pnpm data:realism
pnpm data:settlements
pnpm data:transit
pnpm data:lifelines
pnpm data:water
pnpm data:facilities
pnpm data:telecom
pnpm data:air-quality
pnpm data:earthquakes
pnpm data:waterinfo
pnpm data:gas
pnpm dev
```

Then open the local URL printed by Vite. The generated municipality dataset is checked into `apps/web/public/data`, so subsequent starts do not require a data refresh.

Useful checks:

```bash
pnpm test
pnpm build
pnpm test:e2e
pnpm data:validate
pnpm check
```

## Controls

- Choose a map mode in the left rail.
- Keep **Affected regions** on while changing map modes to see the current T+0–T+72 impact footprint independently of the selected statistic.
- Toggle **Traffic + accidents** to show the server-refreshed NDW accident, congestion and incident records on any map; publication time and live/fallback state are shown in the map panel.
- Switch between PDOK **Map/Aerial/Realistic** and **2D/3D** views. Aerial means an annual Dutch orthophoto, not live satellite imagery. Realistic applies a Netherlands solar-position model to the scenario date, season and local T+0 clock, adds aggregate settlement and BAG building-light proxies, and darkens each municipality as simulated electricity availability falls. Runs without a reference date visibly use a representative seasonal day. 3D loads official PDOK terrain and buildings only after moving to city scale.
- Toggle **Traffic cameras** to exercise the permission-safe camera contract. Until an authorized inventory/media adapter is configured, the UI deliberately reports Unknown and draws no invented cameras.
- Drag the timeline or use the mouse wheel over it for 15-minute changes.
- Use Left/Right outside the map for one-frame steps and Space to play/pause.
- Select a municipality from the header or click it on the map.
- Zoom beyond the city threshold to enter local detail; zoom out to return to the Netherlands.
- Choose a scenario and adjust the physical/context controls in Scenario Lab.
- Open **Advanced** to vary operational assumptions and inspect the resulting drivers in the “Why this state?” trace.
- Use “Compare with” to hold the event constant while comparing another season.
- Click a CBS buurt for its delivery, reserve, official regional statistics, impact, and upstream cause.
- Open **Analytics** for the full modeled history, all official buurt distributions, comparison graphs, accessible data tables and the model-reading guide.

- Issued weather warnings: [KNMI `waarschuwingen_nederland_48h` v1.0](https://data.overheid.nl/dataset/84211-dutch-weather-warnings-from-knmi), continually published in TXT/XML for the coming 48 hours under CC BY 4.0; cached as non-causal province context with strict valid-time and Unknown/fallback semantics.
- Short-horizon precipitation forecast: [KNMI Seamless ensemble probabilities v1.0](https://data.overheid.nl/dataset/7fe16baf-b4b4-4ce8-b146-c01a1b77634c), a CC BY 4.0 pilot WMS with 1 km, five-minute exceedance probabilities for six thresholds through +6 hours. The deployed adapter caches the 3 mm/h exceedance-probability field at T+1/T+3/T+6 with `TIME`, `DIM_REFERENCE_TIME` and `DIM_THRESHOLD`, then stops rather than imply a T+72 product; transparent pixels remain NoData unless explicitly encoded as zero.
- Short-range weather-forecast candidate: [KNMI HARMONIE-AROME Cy43 P2A ensemble v1.0](https://english.knmidata.nl/open-data/harmonie), hourly near-surface grids under CC BY 4.0. KNMI documents forecast leads only through +60 hours; “the last 72 hours retained” describes file availability, not a +72-hour forecast. Its five-member batches are staggered by run hour, so a coherent 30-member construction has about a 54-hour common window. A selective, checksum-bound ingest must preserve cycle, member/statistic, parameter/level/unit, grid, valid hour and NoData. T+60–T+72 stays Unknown unless a separately governed longer-range product is bound to the exercise clock; the national six-day outlook is text context, not a gridded substitute.
- Inland-waterway geometry and registered bridges/locks: [Rijkswaterstaat FIS-VNDS Fairway](https://data.overheid.nl/dataset/42643-fis-vnds---fairway---lijnen), rendered from ArcGIS MapServer layers 55, 3 and 19; catalogue updated 15 April 2026, CC0. Static geometry does not establish present or scenario-time navigability, restriction, operation or capacity; operational Notices to Skippers require a separately timestamped adapter.
- Current precipitation context: [KNMI radar reflectivity composites 2.0](https://dataplatform.knmi.nl/dataset/access/radar-reflectivity-composites-2-0), latest default-time WMS rendering at a 5-minute cadence, cached once per deployment; CC BY 4.0.
- Recent seismic-event context: [KNMI RDSA FDSN Event service](https://rdsa.knmi.nl/fdsnws/event/1/), queried once per deployment for earthquake and induced-event origin points in the preceding 365 days. The RDSA default licence is CC BY 4.0 where no dataset-specific terms apply; preliminary/revisable source parameters remain non-causal and are not a ground-motion product.
- Current air-quality station context: [RIVM Luchtmeetnet open data](https://data.rivm.nl/data/luchtmeetnet/) and its [open API](https://api.luchtmeetnet.nl/open_api), selecting each station's latest PM2.5 observation in a six-hour deployment window. Missing horizon or municipal coverage is NoData, and the layer is non-causal until a governed event/cycle binding exists.
- Water defences: [HWH/PDOK Keringen IMWA](https://api.pdok.nl/hwh/waterschappen-keringen-imwa/ogc/v1?f=html), CC BY-SA 4.0; object status is register context, not guaranteed performance in an event.
- Significant flood-risk screening areas: [Rijkswaterstaat/PDOK risk areas](https://api.pdok.nl/rws/overstromingen-risicogebied/ogc/v1?f=html), based on the 2018 report for ROR second cycle 2016–2021, CC0 and no planned refresh; this does not provide current scenario depth, arrival or duration.
- National pluvial scenario context: [Rijkswaterstaat Klimaateffectatlas inundation depth after 70 mm in two hours](https://data.overheid.nl/dataset/3d9640b8-a6ae-449c-88dd-0ab186094fae), a CC0 WMS/GeoTIFF whose catalogue was updated 5 August 2026. It is a modeled scenario raster, not an observation, local calibration or forecast, and never drives the simulator directly.
- Ground elevation context: [Rijkswaterstaat/PDOK AHN4 DTM](https://data.overheid.nl/dataset/47567-actueel-hoogtebestand-nederland--ahn--dtm), 0.5 m CC0 terrain acquired in 2020–2022 and served through WMS/WCS. Non-ground objects are filtered and the raster must not be interpreted as water depth, hydraulic connectivity, current passability or event state.
- Public-order, safety and administration service locations: [KVK governmental services via PDOK](https://api.pdok.nl/kvk/overheidsdiensten-geharmoniseerd/ogc/v1?f=html), Public Domain Mark 1.0.
- Emergency-department and acute-obstetric roles: [RIVM Vragenlijst aanbod SEH’s en Acute Verloskunde 2026 v1.1](https://datacatalogus.rivm.nl/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.21945/8E767B2D-9CED-4A85-A95D-3AC1A608C663), published 3 July 2026 under CC0; locations are matched to BAG through PDOK Locatieserver.
- Active education-location uses and current school/branch recognitions: [DUO RIO nfo, po, vo, vavo, mbo en ho](https://onderwijsdata.duo.nl/dataset/c416acd1-e083-4ec6-9203-3b20f98fe143), published daily after approximately 06:15 Europe/Amsterdam under Creative Commons Attribution; the publisher does not state a licence version.
- Electricity cables/stations and gas pipes: [Liander Open Data](https://www.liander.nl/over-ons/open-data), current ArcGIS Feature Services, CC BY 4.0. These cover Liander's service area only and are queried as official context, not outage telemetry or a national network.
- Electricity lines and gas-replacement context: [Stedin Open Data](https://www.stedin.net/zakelijk/open-data/liggingsdata-kabels-en-leidingen), queried from the public ArcGIS Feature Service only for the visible map extent. Service metadata states July 2026 data and annual publication. Stedin permits free reuse but does not name a standard licence; the gas layer describes its replacement programme, not all pipes.
- National overhead high-voltage lines: [RIVM public grid map](https://data.overheid.nl/dataset/83491-bovengrondse-hoogspanningslijnen-met-rekenafstanden), 12,348 public WFS line records rendered through its WMS under Public Domain Mark 1.0. The underlying network situation is explicitly November 2023; later catalogue timestamps do not make it current. It is overhead-line context only, not connected topology, load, switching, outage, capacity or restoration state.
- Zuid-Holland electricity-station atlas: [Provincie Zuid-Holland 2025 Liander and Stedin station layers](https://opendata.zuid-holland.nl/geonetwork/srv/dut/catalog.search#/metadata/b9a933f4-ab25-46e2-a7e4-afb73f1039b9), published 24 June 2026 and metadata-updated 13 August 2026 under Public Domain Mark 1.0. The province-scoped WMS is non-causal location context only; it supplies no topology, voltage, load, switching, outage, capacity or restoration state, and empty/outside-province results never mean no station.
- Electricity transport-capacity and queue samples: [Netbeheer Nederland capacity map](https://www.netbeheernederland.nl/netcapaciteit-en-flexibiliteit/capaciteitskaart), deployment-cached from its public frontend service-area endpoints. Capacity/projects refresh at least quarterly and queues monthly; one sampled postcode per municipality is planning context for large-user applications, not emergency service availability.
- Electricity, gas, drinking-water, sewer and fixed-media provider territories: [Stichting Mijn Aansluiting](https://wiki.service.mijnaansluiting.nl/wiki/spaces/Wiki/pages/693731329) live WMS/WFS, catalogued as Public Domain. Provider and record timestamps are queried at the selected municipality; sewer/media coverage can be partial or overlapping.
- Regulated external-safety activities, attention areas, hazardous-substance pipeline references and Basisnet locations: [Rijkswaterstaat Register Externe Veiligheidsrisico's WMS](https://www.registerexterneveiligheid.nl/kaartbeelden). Tiles are requested live on their relevant maps and remain non-causal register context. The WMS capabilities state no fees/access constraints but do not publish a fixed cadence, per-feature timestamp or reuse-licence version; those fields remain unknown instead of being invented.

Refresh scripts embed source URLs, editions, licences and retrieval time. Public water registers, provider territories, and Liander/Stedin geometry are explicitly `official-context`; the RWS cloudburst raster is `official-scenario` context; and NDW's live/fallback current picture plus the KNMI radar/FDSN and RIVM Luchtmeetnet snapshots are `observed-context`. Each retains its coverage/time warnings and `affectsSimulation=false`. Operational states remain synthetic where public, authoritative Dutch utility data is unavailable, fragmented or security-sensitive.

Simulation state is independent from the selected visual layer. National and city views share the same gateway delivery totals, stable region IDs, scenario time, and intervention state. The local view distributes a municipality's national inflow across population- and vulnerability-weighted CBS buurten without creating supply.

The map's animated NWB corridors are logical Mobility and road-logistics/Supply transfers only. Power, drinking-water, drainage, gas, telecom and facility values no longer traverse road edges; until service-specific authorized topology is available, they remain transparent municipality-level synthetic indices. Public operator geometry stays non-causal context.

## Current limitations

- This is a scenario-exploration prototype, not a calibrated digital twin.
- PDOK Luchtfoto RGB is annual Dutch aerial orthophotography, not live satellite imagery. PDOK 3D Basisvoorziening is also annual context; building/terrain geometry does not prove occupancy, damage, access or current condition.
- Realistic lighting is a scenario-clock visualization, not observed cloud cover, night-light imagery, individual lamp telemetry or proof of an outage. Municipality darkness and building/settlement lights are transparent renderings of the synthetic electricity state; unsupported network topology remains Unknown.
- NDW labels its Actueel beeld as a real-time feed. Gatekeeper now retrieves and filters that feed server-side, holds a 60-second last-known-good response, and the open page requests a refresh every minute while the overlay is in use. If the upstream fetch fails, the UI visibly changes to stale/fallback semantics; this is not a guaranteed mission-critical feed or a historical/future scenario replay. Missing or expired records stay Unknown and never mean an open road.
- The traffic-camera UI is an adapter boundary, not a camera feed. Rijkswaterstaat does not make operational road-camera imagery generally available to private third parties without a legal basis, and NDW currently describes camera support in its viewer as under development. No external stream URL or credential is shipped to the browser.
- Hazard forcing and fragility curves are deterministic model assumptions, not calibrated forecasts. Official flood-depth rasters and representative KNMI time series are a later calibration step.
- Real roads/public-transport lines do not imply real capacity: lane counts, measured traffic, closures, vehicle capacity, and emergency-routing behavior are not yet calibrated.
- Current NDW situations are publication-timestamped operational observations, not a forecast for an arbitrary simulated event. They remain display-only until location, direction and route matching are validated. The sourced N3 Papendrechtsebrug project restriction demonstrates a full valid-time closure; the A27 Merwedebrug, N210 Algerabrug, Lage Rijndijk/Spanjaardsbrug and N280 Kelperbrug records demonstrate class/role-specific restrictions that remain non-numeric until vehicle-mix evidence exists. Lingewaard’s Rijndijk record demonstrates an exact internal context corridor, while Lisse’s planned underpass transition and Maasgouw’s pedestrian/cycle-only Stuw Linne closure remain card-only until an exact typed asset and confirmation exist. None is extrapolated beyond its verified interval, and absence never proves a road open.
- The cached KNMI radar image is a near-real-time observation, not a forecast and not synchronized to an arbitrary scenario start. It remains display-only until a selected historical/forecast weather sequence is explicitly bound to the 72-hour scenario clock.
- The KNMI Seamless overlay is an experimental exceedance probability, not deterministic rainfall. It ends at T+6 and remains display-only. A checksum-bound HARMONIE cycle can provide candidate gridded weather only through about T+60, not T+72; the final twelve hours require a separately governed longer-range product or remain Unknown. Absent forecast coverage is never zero weather or an all-clear.
- Waterinfo levels and discharge are latest point observations, not a spatial flood model or guarantee of current quality. Station timestamps can be delayed or stale, publisher fault flags are preserved, offshore/boundary points may not map to a municipality, and lack of a municipal point is no-data rather than zero or safe.
- Waterinfo forecast points are issued guidance, but the rolling-map response is not automatically the exercise forecast: it lacks a single published cycle issue time and its point coverage declines by lead. Requested window, response time, valid time and response hash are retained; +48/+72 gaps and municipalities without contained points stay Unknown/NoData rather than zero. The cached guidance remains display-only until a selected event binds station boundary conditions to the scenario clock.
- BRO groundwater WMS markers expose registrations and characteristic metadata, not full GLD measurements, current groundwater level, a continuous surface, event threshold or forecast. They are display-only until selected GLD series are retrieved with observation time, quality and vertical-reference semantics and are explicitly bound to a scenario.
- Public water-control and wastewater facilities are visible as register context, but their operational state, dependency graph and usable emergency capacity remain unknown. Liander cable/station/pipe geometry and Stedin electricity/gas-replacement geometry are visible only in their respective territories at bounded map scales; neither supplies connected switching/valve topology, pressure/flow, capacity, outage or restoration state. Enexis and smaller DSO geometry are not yet rendered.
- ROR risk areas and registered water defences improve spatial orientation but cannot calibrate local impact without an expert-selected LIWO/water-board mechanism, defence state, depth/velocity, arrival and recession layer.
- KVK governmental-service points use only two coarse INSPIRE categories. RIVM supplies dated SEH/acute-obstetric roles, and DUO RIO supplies current registered education-location uses, but neither establishes live occupancy, diversion, attendance, shelter activation, staffing, generator endurance or incident capacity. Other typed facility and shelter adapters remain planned.
- NWB road corridors are limited to Mobility and explicitly assumed road logistics/Supply. Non-road services use no NWB transfer edge; their municipality-level synthetic indices remain a calibration fallback until service-specific, security-reviewed topology and state are available.
- “Modeled residents under pressure” is a current post-network equivalent count; “local pre-network deprived person-hours” is a cumulative matched-baseline integral that excludes intermunicipal transfers. The interface names both scopes because zero in the latter can coexist with nonzero current pressure and is not contradictory evidence.
- BAG woonplaatsen are official named administrative address areas, not necessarily built-up town/village footprints.
- Route capacity uses generalized simulation units (`u/h`), not MW, m³/h, vehicles/h, or operator measurements.
- KNMI climate controls use transparent scenario-path transforms; they do not claim a probability or official warning.
- The current GTFS feed has a finite validity period. A historical summer/winter timetable comparison needs archived feeds; the season control currently changes hazard and demand context.
- “Modeled residents under service pressure” is an equivalent count derived from a transparent service-pressure curve, not an observed or identified group of people.
- Analytics uses 25 full-network states at three-hour intervals for responsiveness; the interactive map itself retains 289 quarter-hour states.

## Why 72 hours

The project meeting framed a prolonged power outage as a cascade rather than a single utility failure: payments, food cooling, medical records, fuel access, traffic control, telecom, water pressure, sanitation, emergency response, and public information degrade on different clocks. The first 72 hours are therefore modeled as a sequence of changing dependencies and scarcity decisions—not one static damage map.
