Opening: this project asks what happens after the first shock—when essential services begin to depend on one another. It is an interactive exploration prototype, not a forecast or calibrated digital twin.

Problem framing 02

The premise

A disaster is not one failure.

It is a chain of dependencies, delays and constrained choices.

01

Shock

Wind, water, heat, cyber, ground motion or fire creates the initiating pressure.

02

Cascade

Power, telecom, water, mobility and supply degrade on different clocks.

03

Choice

Preparedness, backup, access and repair capacity change what happens next.

The simulator is about the cascade. It steps through how service availability, reserves, repair and transport access interact—not just how a hazard colours a map.

Causal

Scenario controls and model inputs directly affect the simulation.

Structural

Geography and audited connectivity allocate state and supported flows.

Context

Timestamped official observations orient the user, but remain non-causal by default.

Unknown

Missing support stays visible. It never silently becomes zero impact or open access.

Every refresh preserves source URL, edition, licence, retrieval time and caveats.

This is the key data-governance rule. A current road incident or radar image is not automatically a future scenario. It needs explicit time, place and semantics before it can affect the engine.

Critical reflection 12

Useful because the limits stay visible

Designed to fail safely.

245of 342 municipalities audited

Unsupported local pathways are withheld instead of rendered as confident detail.

What it does well

  • Compare assumptions and policies
  • Reveal time-dependent cascades
  • Keep national and local views coherent
  • Expose drivers and counterfactuals
  • Preserve provenance and uncertainty

What it does not claim

  • A calibrated digital twin or forecast
  • Live utility outage telemetry
  • Physical utility network topology
  • Observed affected-person counts
  • Real-world units for route capacity

Audit snapshot generated 3 September 2026. Human review remains necessary.

The municipality audit program checks numerical and spatial plausibility and compiles deterministic guards. Audit prose never becomes a causal model automatically.
